
SHOPEASE RETURN POLICY

1. Return Window
   Customers can return eligible products within 15 calendar days of delivery.

2. Product Condition
   Products must be unused, undamaged, and returned with their original packaging and accessories.

3. Non-Returnable Products
   Personal-care products, customized products, and products marked as non-returnable cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged or defective.

4. Damaged or Defective Products
   Customers who receive damaged or defective products should contact ShopEase support within 48 hours of delivery and provide photographs when possible.

5. Return Request
   Customers can initiate a return by contacting ShopEase customer support with their order ID and reason for return.

6. Return Approval
   All return requests are subject to eligibility checks. Submitting a return request does not guarantee approval.

7. Refund Processing
   Once an approved return is received and inspected, the refund is processed according to the ShopEase refund policy.

Note: ShopEase is a fictional company created for this portfolio project.
