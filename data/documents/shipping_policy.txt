
SHOPEASE SHIPPING POLICY

1. Standard Delivery
   Standard shipping usually takes 3 to 7 business days from the date the order is shipped.

2. Express Delivery
   Express shipping usually takes 1 to 3 business days from the date the order is shipped, where this service is available.

3. Order Processing
   Orders are generally processed within 1 to 2 business days before shipment. Processing time may vary during holidays or promotional periods.

4. Order Tracking
   Once an order has shipped, customers can use the tracking number provided by ShopEase to check the shipment status.

5. Delayed Shipments
   Delivery estimates are not guarantees. Weather, carrier disruptions, public holidays, and other unexpected events may delay delivery.

6. Missing Tracking Information
   If an order is still processing, a tracking number may not yet be available. Customers should check their order status before contacting support.

7. Delivery Issues
   If a shipment appears lost or tracking information has not updated for an extended period, customers should contact ShopEase support with their order ID.

8. Delivery Address
   Customers should verify their delivery address before placing an order. Address changes after shipment may not be possible.

9. Shipping Charges
   Applicable shipping charges are displayed during checkout before the customer confirms the purchase.

Note: ShopEase is a fictional company created for this portfolio project.
