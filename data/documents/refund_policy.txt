
SHOPEASE REFUND POLICY

1. Refund Eligibility
   Refunds are available for eligible orders after a return is approved or when another refund condition specified by ShopEase applies.

2. Refund Processing Time
   After an approved returned product has been received and inspected, ShopEase initiates the refund within 5 to 7 business days.

3. Bank Processing Time
   After ShopEase initiates a refund, the bank or payment provider may require an additional 5 to 10 business days to show the amount in the customer's account.

4. Original Payment Method
   Refunds are generally issued to the original payment method. If that method is unavailable, the customer should contact ShopEase support for assistance.

5. Cancelled Orders
   If a cancelled order qualifies for a refund, the refund is initiated according to the applicable refund processing rules.

6. Refund Status
   Customers can contact ShopEase support with their order ID to check their refund status.

7. Delayed Refunds
   If a refund has not arrived within the expected timeframe, the customer should contact ShopEase support with the order ID and available payment or refund details.

8. Refund Approval
   A refund request is subject to eligibility checks. Requesting a refund does not guarantee approval.

Note: ShopEase is a fictional company created for this portfolio project.
